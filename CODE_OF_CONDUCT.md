# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to keep technical discussion professional and useful.

- Critique code, measurements, architecture, and claims — not people.
- Provide reproducible evidence where possible.
- Do not harass, threaten, insult, or discriminate against contributors.
- Do not deliberately derail issues or repeatedly post unsupported claims as fact.
- Respect maintainer decisions on scope, especially around unsupported games, anti-cheat environments, and
  unverified performance claims.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove disruptive content and may restrict participation when necessary.

For serious conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately rather than escalating the issue publicly.
